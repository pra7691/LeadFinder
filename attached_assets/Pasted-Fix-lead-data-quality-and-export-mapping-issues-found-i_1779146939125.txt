PART 5 — PREVENT CROSS-DOMAIN EMAIL LEAKAGE
===========================================

Current issue:
A lead can show an unrelated email from another company/domain.

Example:
chelsea-apps.com showing [holla@3sidedcube.com](mailto:holla@3sidedcube.com)

Fix:
Email extraction must be isolated per crawled lead/domain.

Rules:

* reset email/phone extraction arrays for every domain crawl
* never reuse extraction context across leads
* prefer emails matching the lead root domain
* if an email domain differs from the lead root domain, do not include it unless it is clearly found on that lead’s own website page
* flag external-domain emails as warning

Add field if practical:
email_domain_status

Values:

* matching_domain
* external_domain
* mixed
* none

Outreach readiness:

* external_domain and mixed should show warning
* do not auto-approve such leads
