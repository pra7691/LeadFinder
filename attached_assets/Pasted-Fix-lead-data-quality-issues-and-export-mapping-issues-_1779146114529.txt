=================================================

Current issue:
The system mixes searched country and extracted company country.

Add/ensure two separate fields:

1. target_country
   Meaning:
   The country used in the campaign search query.
   Example:
   USA

2. company_country
   Meaning:
   The country extracted from company website/address/phone.
   Example:

* target_country should come from source_country/search country.

PART 5 — PREVENT CROSS-DOMAIN EMAIL LEAKAGE
===========================================

Current issue:
A lead may receive an email from a different unrelated domain.

Example:
chelsea-apps.com lead showing:
[holla@3sidedcube.com](mailto:holla@3sidedcube.com)

This should not happen unless the domains are intentionally related.

Rules:
For each lead:

* prefer emails matching the lead root domain
* allow generic external email only if found on the company’s own website and clearly presented as contact
* flag external-domain emails as “external_email_warning”
* do not mix emails between different leads/crawls
* reset extraction context for each domain crawl
* never reuse email arrays across crawls

Add field if practical:
email_domain_status:

* matching_domain
* external_domain
* mixed
* none

In outreach readiness:
external-domain emails should show warning.
