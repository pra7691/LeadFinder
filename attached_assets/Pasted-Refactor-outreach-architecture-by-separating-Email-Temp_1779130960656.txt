Refactor outreach architecture by separating Email Templates from Campaigns and adding OpenAI settings.

Do not build automated email sending yet unless already partially built.
Do not remove existing leads or campaigns.
Preserve current data where possible.

Current issue:
Email subject/body is stored inside Campaigns, but campaigns should only manage lead discovery. Email templates should be reusable and selectable when launching outreach to a List.

New structure:
- Campaign = discovery strategy
- Lead = company/person record
- List = curated group of full lead records
- Email Template = reusable subject/body/prompt
- Outreach = selected List + selected Email Template + selected sender account

==================================================

Remove or hide these fields from Campaign create/edit UI:
- email subject
- email body/template

If database fields already exist, do not break migration. Keep old fields nullable or deprecated if needed, but do not use them for new outreach.

- objective

- countries
- min relevance score
- max searches/day

  - website
  - country
  - relevance reason

  - template subject
