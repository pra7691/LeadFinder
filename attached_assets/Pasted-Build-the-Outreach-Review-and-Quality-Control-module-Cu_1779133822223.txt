Build the Outreach Review and Quality Control module.

Current system status:

* discovery works
* lists work
* templates work
* AI personalization exists
* outreach queue exists

But there is no strong human review layer before sending.

This is dangerous for deliverability and lead quality.

Goal:
Create a professional review workflow before emails are sent.

Do not build full automation yet.
Do not auto-send emails.

==================================================
PART 1 — OUTREACH REVIEW SCREEN
===============================

Add a dedicated page:
Outreach Review

Purpose:
Review queued outreach drafts before sending.

Show table/cards with:

* company name
* recipient email
* sender account
* selected template
* generated subject
* generated preview snippet
* qualification status
* relevance score
* campaign
* list
* AI personalized or not
* queue status

Statuses:

* draft
* pending_review
* approved_to_send
* rejected
* sent
* failed

Default newly generated outreach:
pending_review

=======================

Actions:

* approve
* reject
* edit subject
* edit body
* regenerate with AI
* preview full email
* bulk approve
* bulk reject

Rules:

* only approved_to_send can later be sent
* rejected cannot be sent

==================================================
PART 3 — EMAIL QUALITY WARNINGS
===============================

Add warning indicators:

* no personalization
* missing company name
* suspicious generic email
* missing unsubscribe text
* low relevance lead
* no website
* no country
* duplicate email recipient
* risky domain

Display quality badges/colors.

==================================================
PART 4 — DELIVERABILITY PROTECTION
==================================

Add first-level protections:

1. Duplicate protection
   Do not queue duplicate recipient email for same campaign/list/template combination.

2. Cooldown tracking
   Track last_contacted_at per lead.

3. Sending limits
   Show warnings if:

* sender daily limit nearly reached
* too many emails queued

4. Domain health warnings
   Add warning if:

* sender domain differs heavily from campaign niche
* too many pending sends
